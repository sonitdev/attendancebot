# Master Plan

The original user-approved master plan defines product scope. This repository's implementation interpretation is maintained in the documents below.

## Product outcome

Reliable, auditable evidence of who attended a physical project site, when they arrived and left, whether location was credible, and separately documented work assessments by authorized people.

## MVP boundary

Included: organizations, employees, projects, sites, schedules, assignments, verified Telegram linkage, GPS check-in/out, attendance events/records, admin visibility, daily/weekly reporting, audit history.

Excluded from MVP: payroll, overtime, QR/NFC/biometric attendance, PWA/native apps, continuous tracking, AI summaries and advanced analytics.

The detailed requirements and acceptance criteria are in `PRODUCT_REQUIREMENTS.md` and `TEST_PLAN.md`.
